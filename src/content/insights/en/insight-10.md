The answer, in most cases, is **no**.

Today, many people can form an LLC in the United States completely remotely, without needing a visa, residency, or physical presence in the country.

However, there are certain important aspects you should know before starting the process.

In this article, we will explain how LLC formation really works for foreigners, which steps can be done remotely, and in which cases traveling to the U.S. might be useful.

## Can a foreigner open an LLC in the United States?

Yes.

The United States allows people who are not U.S. citizens or residents to own an LLC.

In fact, thousands of international entrepreneurs use LLCs to:

- Sell services abroad.
- Operate digital businesses.
- Earn in dollars.
- Set up international corporate structures.
- Access the U.S. financial system.
- Expand global operations.

You do not need to have:

- A Green Card.
- An investor visa.
- A U.S. Social Security Number.
- U.S. residency.

## So, can everything be done remotely?

In many cases, yes.

Currently, most of the process can be done online or through authorized representation.

### Processes that can normally be done remotely

**LLC formation:** Company incorporation can be done remotely in most states. This includes state registration, obtaining corporate documents, and appointing a registered agent.

**Obtaining an EIN from the IRS:** The EIN (Employer Identification Number) is the company's tax number. Even if the owner is a foreigner with no SSN, the EIN can typically be requested from the IRS.

**Corporate documentation:** Many documents can be prepared digitally: Operating Agreement, corporate resolutions, internal records, and corporate certificates.

**Opening certain fintechs or financial platforms:** Depending on the case and provider, some platforms allow remote verification processes. However, this depends on nationality, business model, risk profile, and each institution's internal policies.

## When might traveling to the U.S. be useful?

Although not always required, in some cases traveling can facilitate certain processes.

### In-person bank account opening

Some traditional banks may require physical presence, in-person verification, and original documentation. Especially if the company will handle high amounts, the business model is complex, there is physical U.S. operation, or the bank requires additional validations.

### Physical operation in the U.S.

If the business will have an office, employees, inventory, commercial space, or active operations within the U.S., traveling could be recommended to coordinate certain operational aspects.

### Business relationships or investment

Many entrepreneurs also travel to meet with suppliers, establish banking relationships, seek investors, sign contracts, or explore business opportunities.

## Common mistake: believing an LLC grants residency or a visa

One of the biggest myths is thinking that opening an LLC automatically grants residency, generates a visa, allows you to live legally in the U.S., or provides a work permit.

**This is incorrect.** An LLC is a business structure, not an immigration status.

Although it can be part of certain immigration or investment strategies, that depends on completely separate processes.

## What does a foreigner need to open an LLC?

Normally required:

- Valid passport.
- Personal address.
- Business information.
- State where you want to incorporate.
- Registered agent.

Depending on the case, proof of business activity, banking information, KYC documentation, and additional verifications may also be requested.

## What is the best state to open an LLC if I am a foreigner?

It depends on several factors such as business type, physical presence in the U.S. or not, banking needs, commercial objectives, privacy, and maintenance costs.

The most commonly used states are:

| State | Main advantage |
|-------|----------------|
| **Wyoming** | Low costs, privacy, simple process |
| **Florida** | Commercial proximity to LATAM |
| **Delaware** | Corporate reputation, ideal for holdings |

Each has different advantages depending on the operating model.

## Will an LLC help me earn in dollars?

In many cases, yes. One of the main reasons Latin American entrepreneurs open LLCs is to access business accounts, fintech platforms, payment processors, and international collection tools. This can facilitate global operations and improve the professional image of the business.

## Important: opening the LLC is just the beginning

Many people think the process ends when the company is incorporated. But afterward there are important obligations such as state reports, renewals, informational filings, tax compliance, and corporate maintenance.

A poorly managed LLC can result in fines, banking problems, tax risks, and state suspensions.

## Conclusion

In most cases, **you do not need to travel to the United States to open an LLC**. Currently, most of the process can be done remotely, allowing Latin American entrepreneurs to operate internationally without needing to physically reside in the U.S.

However, each case must be analyzed individually to correctly choose the state, structure, tax obligations, banking strategy, and corporate maintenance.
