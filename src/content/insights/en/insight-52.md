**Stripe** continues to be the preferred payment platform for digital businesses worldwide. In 2026, the platform updated its verification, compliance, and documentation requirements, making the account opening process more rigorous — but also more secure.

In this guide, we explain all the **requirements to open and maintain a Stripe account in 2026**, including new verification rules, accepted documents, and compliance obligations.

## 1. What's New with Stripe in 2026

### Key Changes

| Change | Description | Impact |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| **Enhanced identity verification** | Stripe now requires video selfie or biometrics for high-risk accounts | More security, slower process |
| **BOI mandatory** | Beneficial Ownership Information required for all US business accounts | New businesses must file |
| **Expanded country support** | Stripe added more Asian and African countries | More options, but LATAM still limited |
| **Updated fees** | New charges for international cards and currency conversion | Slightly higher costs |
| **Risk ML** | Stripe uses advanced AI to detect fraud and high-risk businesses | Fewer false positives, but more rigor |
| **Stripe Identity 2.0** | New version of the identity verification service | Mandatory verification in more cases |

> **Note:** Stripe's verification rules in 2026 are stricter than ever. Having the correct documentation from the start is essential to avoid blocks.

## 2. Basic Requirements to Create an Account

### For Individuals (Sole Proprietor)

| Requirement | Details |
|------------|---------|
| **Identity document** | Valid passport or government-issued ID (with photo) |
| **Proof of address** | Utility bill or bank statement (less than 3 months old) |
| **Date of birth** | Over 18 years old |
| **Phone number** | Valid and verifiable |
| **Email** | Cannot be temporary (Gmail, Outlook, etc. are accepted) |
| **Website or online presence** | Site, app, online store, or marketplace profile |
| **Bank account** | In the country where the Stripe account will be registered |
| **Selfie/Facial verification** | Required in 2026 for most accounts |

### For Businesses

| Requirement | Details |
|------------|---------|
| **Company registration** | Certificate of incorporation or equivalent |
| **EIN (US) or equivalent** | Business tax identification number |
| **Business address** | Physical address (not PO Box in many cases) |
| **Beneficial owner documents** | ID for all owners with over 25% stake |
| **Business bank account** | In the company's name |
| **Company proof of address** | Utility bill or lease agreement |
| **Website or app** | With contact information and policies |

## 3. Accepted and Restricted Business Types

### Automatically Supported Businesses

- E-commerce and online stores
- Subscriptions and SaaS
- Professional services (consulting, design, development)
- Online education and courses
- Software and apps
- Digital marketing and advertising
- Subscription services (boxes, content)

### Businesses Requiring Manual Review

- Travel and bookings (hotels, flights)
- Financial services (consulting, currency exchange)
- Health and wellness (supplements, medical equipment)
- Regulated products (alcohol, tobacco — where permitted)

### PROHIBITED Businesses

Stripe strictly prohibits in 2026:

- **Cryptocurrencies** (except licensed exchanges in specific countries)
- **Gambling and betting** (including online casinos)
- **Counterfeit products** and replicas
- **Weapons, ammunition, and explosives**
- **Drugs and controlled substances** (including CBD/THC in most countries)
- **Adult content** (pornography, onlyfans, etc.)
- **Get-rich-quick schemes** (multi-level marketing, pyramids)
- **Sale of fake documents or certificates**
- **Hacking or data breach services**

> **Tip:** If your business is in a risk area, consider alternative platforms or contact Stripe before creating an account to verify eligibility.

## 4. Verification Process in 2026

### Stage 1: Basic Information

- Name, email, phone
- Country of operation
- Business type and description

### Stage 2: Documentation

- Upload identity documents (front and back)
- Upload proof of address
- Upload company documents (if applicable)

### Stage 3: Identity Verification (Stripe Identity 2.0)

In 2026, Stripe uses:

- **Video selfie verification** — record a short video saying your name
- **Biometric validation** — facial comparison with the document
- **Live document verification** — photo of the document in real time
- **Government database cross-check** — in supported countries

**Estimated time:** 5 to 15 minutes for verification

### Stage 4: Bank Account Linking

- Micro-deposits (2 random deposits to the bank account)
- Login-based verification (Stripe Instant Bank Verification)
- Manual confirmation with bank statement

### Stage 5: Risk Analysis

- Stripe analyzes the business model
- Verifies the website or application
- Evaluates the entrepreneur's history (if any)
- Decides whether the account can operate or needs additional review

> **Total timeframe:** 24 to 72 hours for simple accounts; 1 to 4 weeks for complex or high-risk accounts.

### Compliance

- **BOI (Beneficial Ownership Information):** Mandatory for all US companies since 2024
- **Enhanced KYC:** Stripe requests additional documents if it detects risk profile changes
- **AML (Anti-Money Laundering):** Stripe monitors suspicious transactions and may block accounts
- **Sanctions Screening:** Stripe checks all transactions against international sanctions lists

## 8. Common Opening Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Illegible Documents

- Blurry or reflective photos
- Cut-off documents (missing corners)
- Inadequate lighting

**Solution:** Use a scanner or take clear photos with good lighting, plain background, and well-framed document.

### Mistake 2: Inconsistent Information

- Different name across documents
- Address doesn't match proof
- Wrong date of birth

**Solution:** Verify all documents and information are consistent before submitting.

### Mistake 3: Incomplete Website or App

- Site under construction
- No privacy policy or terms of service
- No contact information
- No clear product/service description

**Solution:** Have a complete, professional website with all required pages before registering.

### Mistake 4: Incompatible Bank Account

- Personal account for business
- Account in different country from Stripe account
- Account at a bank not accepted by Stripe

**Solution:** Open a business bank account in the same country where the Stripe account is registered.
