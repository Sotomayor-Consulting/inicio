**Protecting digital assets** is the process of securing, safeguarding, and legally structuring your intangible goods: source code, trademarks, domains, customer data, digital content, and crypto assets. In the digital economy, these assets are often worth more than physical ones.

In this guide, we explain **how to protect digital assets** in 2026: intellectual property, technical security, legal structure, insurance, and Stripe's role.

## 1. What Are Digital Assets?

### Types of Digital Assets

- **Intellectual property:** Source code, patents, trademarks, copyright -- High
- **Online assets:** Domains, website, social media, stores -- High
- **Data:** Databases, customer data, analytics -- High
- **Digital content:** Courses, eBooks, templates, designs -- Medium-High
- **Infrastructure:** Servers, cloud accounts, APIs -- Critical
- **Crypto assets:** Bitcoin, Ethereum, NFTs, tokens -- Variable
- **Digital financial assets:** Stripe, PayPal, Wise, online bank accounts -- Critical
### Why They Are Vulnerable

- **Cyber theft:** Account hacking, phishing, malware
- **Loss of access:** Forgotten passwords, death without leaving access
- **Lawsuits:** IP disputes, breach of contract
- **Technical failures:** Data loss, server downtime
- **Regulatory:** Changes in privacy, crypto, IP laws
- **Fraud:** Social engineering, scams, impersonation
> In 2026, digital assets represent over 70% of tech company value. Protecting them is protecting your business.

## 3. Technical Security

### Account Protection

- **Stripe:** 2FA, IP whitelist, limited team
- **Business email:** 2FA, passkeys, secure recovery
- **Cloud (AWS, GCP, Azure):** IAM roles, MFA, rotated access keys

### Digital Asset Backup

- **Source code:** Git + remote repo + offline backup -- Per commit
- **Database:** Automated snapshot + replica -- Daily
- **Stripe configuration:** Export configurations -- Monthly
- **Content:** Cloud + local copy -- Weekly
- **Passwords:** Encrypted export + secure physical copy -- Quarterly

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the source code mine or the company's?

If you created it on your own and there's no assignment agreement, it's yours personally. To protect it, sign a rights assignment to the company.

### How do I protect my Stripe account?

Mandatory 2FA, IP whitelist, limited role-based users, API key rotation, and constant monitoring with Radar.

### Do I need insurance for digital assets?

Yes, especially if you handle customer data or run a SaaS. A cyber attack can cost tens of thousands of dollars.

### What happens if I die without leaving access to my digital assets?

Your heirs likely won't be able to access them. Stripe will freeze the account. Prepare a digital succession plan.

### Does Stripe protect my data?

Stripe is PCI-DSS Level 1. Your data and your customers' data are encrypted and protected. Stripe doesn't share data without authorization.

### Should I register my trademark?

Yes. Without trademark registration, anyone can use your name. Register with USPTO (US) and in countries where you operate.
