## What is Mortgage Pre-Qualification?

**Mortgage pre-qualification** is the first step to obtaining a mortgage loan. It's a process where we evaluate your financial capacity and give you an estimate of the loan you can obtain. This analysis is based on factors such as income, debts, financial history, and available funds.

At **Sotomayor Consulting International**, we facilitate this process so that **in a few days you can know your purchasing capacity and start your property search with confidence.**

## Difference Between Pre-Qualification and Pre-Approval

It's important to differentiate between **pre-qualification** and **pre-approval**:

* **Pre-qualification:** It's a preliminary analysis of your income and payment capacity to estimate the loan amount. It doesn't involve a deep credit check or extensive documentation.
* **Pre-approval:** It's a more detailed process where we review official documents and verify your creditworthiness. With pre-approval, you can **present stronger offers in the real estate market.**

1.  **Official Identification**
    * Valid passport or ID document.
2.  **Proof of Income**
    * Since foreigners don't have a U.S. credit history, we demonstrate their payment capacity with:
    * Bank statements from the last 6 months.
    * Tax returns or financial statements from their home country.

5.  **Information on Current Debts**
