**Stripe** and **PayPal** are the two most popular payment platforms in the world. Together, they process hundreds of billions of dollars annually. But choosing between them depends on your business type, countries of operation, sales volume, and specific needs.

In this detailed comparison, we analyze **Stripe vs PayPal** in 2026 to help you decide which platform is ideal for your business.

## 1. Overview

| Feature | Stripe | PayPal |
|---------|--------|--------|
| **Founded** | 2010 | 1998 |
| **Headquarters** | San Francisco, USA | San Jose, USA |
| **Supported countries** | 40+ | 200+ |
| **Supported currencies** | 135+ | 25+ |
| **Business model** | Payment processor (API) | Digital wallet + processor |
| **Target audience** | Developers and digital businesses | Consumers and general e-commerce |
| **Ease of use** | Requires technical integration | Ready plugins (WordPress, Shopify) |

## 2. Fees and Costs

### Transaction Fees

| Type | Stripe | PayPal |
|------|--------|--------|
| **Online transaction (card)** | 2.9% + $0.30 | 2.99% + $0.49 |
| **International cards** | +1.5% | +1.5% |
| **Currency conversion** | 1% | 2.5% - 4% |
| **PayPal payment** | N/A (doesn't accept PayPal) | 2.99% + $0.49 (same rate) |
| **Chargeback** | $15.00 | $20.00 |
| **Refund** | Free (fee not refunded) | Free (fee not refunded) |

> **Key difference:** PayPal has higher currency conversion and chargeback fees. For businesses with frequent international sales, Stripe is significantly cheaper.

### Hidden Costs

| Hidden Cost | Stripe | PayPal |
|-------------|--------|--------|
| **Bank withdrawal fee** | $0 (ACH); 1% (instant transfer) | $0 (standard); 1% (instant) |
| **Inactivity fee** | $0 | $0 |
| **Cancellation fee** | $0 | $0 |
| **Monthly fixed fee** | $0 | $0 (PayPal Payments Standard); $30/month (PayPal Payments Pro) |

## 3. Supported Countries

### Stripe
- **Total:** 40+ countries
- **Strong presence:** North America, Europe, Oceania
- **Limited presence:** Latin America (only Mexico and Brazil), Asia, Africa

### PayPal
- **Total:** 200+ countries
- **Global presence:** Virtually every country in the world
- **Latin America:** Available in most countries

> **PayPal advantage** for those who need to accept payments from customers in countries where Stripe is not available.

## 6. Security and Fraud Prevention

| Aspect | Stripe | PayPal |
|--------|--------|--------|
| **Fraud** | Stripe Radar (ML, customizable rules) | Basic protection + Seller Protection |

## 9. Stripe + PayPal: Using Both

Many businesses choose to use **both platforms** simultaneously:

### Recommended Strategy

### Advantages of Using Both

- ✅ **More conversions:** Customers choose their preferred method
- ✅ **Redundancy:** If one platform goes down, the other continues
- ✅ **Global reach:** Stripe for supported countries, PayPal for the rest
- ✅ **A/B testing:** Compare conversion rates between platforms

### Disadvantages

- ❌ **Two integrations to maintain**
- ❌ **More complex reconciliation**
- ❌ **Two chargeback fees**
- ❌ **Two accounts to manage**

## 10. Summary: Which to Choose?

### Choose Stripe IF:

- You have a digital business (SaaS, e-commerce, subscriptions)
- You need a robust API and customization
- You operate mainly in countries where Stripe is available
- You want lower fees, especially on currency conversion
- You value on-site checkout experience
- You need advanced features (connect, billing, tax)

### Choose PayPal IF:

- You're just starting and want something simple
- You need to accept global payments (200+ countries)
- Your customers trust the PayPal brand
- You sell on marketplaces (eBay, Etsy)
- You want chargeback protection (Seller Protection)
- You don't have a developer for integration

### Use BOTH IF:

- You want to maximize conversion rate
- You need redundancy and backup
- You serve customers in countries with and without Stripe
- You have enough volume to manage two platforms

## Conclusion

The choice between **Stripe and PayPal** depends on your business profile:

- **Stripe is superior** for digital businesses, SaaS, professional e-commerce, and businesses needing customization and low fees.
- **PayPal is superior** for those just starting, needing immediate global reach, or selling on marketplaces.

The best strategy for most businesses is to **use Stripe as the primary processor and PayPal as a secondary option** — maximizing conversion without depending on a single platform.
