Forming an LLC in the United States has become a very popular option for Latin American entrepreneurs and business owners who want to:

- Earn in dollars.
- Operate internationally.
- Access the U.S. financial system.
- Professionalize their business.
- Expand global operations.

However, many people believe that forming an LLC is complicated or that they need to travel to the U.S. to do it.

The reality is that, in many cases, the process can be done remotely with relatively simple documentation.

In this article, we will explain what documents you need to form an LLC correctly and what information is typically requested during the process.

## Can a foreigner open an LLC?

Yes. The United States allows non-resident aliens to own LLCs.

You do not need to be a U.S. citizen, have a Green Card, have a work visa, or have a Social Security Number (SSN). However, it is important to structure the company correctly from the start.

## Basic documents to form an LLC

Although requirements may vary slightly depending on the state and service provider, you will typically need the following:

### Valid passport

This is usually the main identification document for foreigners. It is requested to verify the owner's identity, nationality, personal information, and KYC data.

**Recommendation:** The passport must be valid, legible, and match exactly the information used in the LLC.

### Personal address

You will normally need to provide a personal residential address. This address **does not necessarily have to be in the United States**. It can be an address in Ecuador, Mexico, Colombia, Peru, Bolivia, or another country of residence. It is used for internal records, tax information, bank verifications, and KYC processes.

### LLC name

You will need to define the company name. The name must be available in the chosen state, must include "LLC," must not infringe on trademarks, and should look professional for banks and clients.

### Business activity information

A basic description of the business is typically requested, for example: digital marketing, consulting, e-commerce, software development, service export, investments, or real estate. This helps structure the LLC correctly, affects banking processes, and influences compliance and taxation.

### Commercial address or Registered Agent

Every LLC must have a registered agent in the state where it is incorporated. This is the person or company authorized to receive legal notices, state correspondence, and official documents. **You do not need to live in the U.S. for this** — the formation service typically includes the registered agent.

### Member or partner information

If the LLC will have a single owner, multiple members, managers, or foreign members, this information must be correctly registered.

### Email and phone number

These are used for state communications, IRS, banks, financial platforms, and compliance.

## Do I need a bank account before forming?

**No.** The typical order is: LLC formation first, then EIN obtainment, then corporate document preparation, and finally bank account opening.

## What documents are obtained after forming?

Once the LLC is formed, you typically obtain:

### Certificate of Formation / Articles of Organization

Official state document confirming the company's existence.

### Operating Agreement

Internal document regulating ownership, membership interests, management, and LLC operations.

### EIN Letter

Document issued by the IRS with the company's tax identification number.

## Do I need to travel to the U.S.?

In many cases, **no**. Most of the process can currently be done remotely. However, depending on the bank or business model, some institutions may require physical presence for certain banking procedures.

## Common mistake: forming an LLC without preparation

Many people incorporate companies without understanding their obligations, analyzing taxation, reviewing the appropriate state, preparing a banking structure, or having clarity on compliance. This can lead to problems with banks, fintech rejections, fines, tax risks, and unnecessary costs.

## Which state is best?

The most commonly used states are Wyoming, Florida, and Delaware. The best option depends on business type, commercial objectives, banking strategy, privacy, maintenance costs, and actual operations.

## How long does it take to form an LLC?

Time may vary depending on the state, processing type, IRS time for the EIN, and documentation provided. Some processes can be quick, while others may take longer depending on the case.

## Document summary

- **Valid passport** — Yes, main identification for foreigners.
- **Personal address** — Yes, can be in your country of residence.
- **LLC name** — Yes, must be available in the state.
- **Business activity** — Yes, basic business description.
- **Registered Agent** — Yes, included in formation services.
- **Member information** — Yes, owner and partner data.
- **Email and phone** — Yes, official communications.

## Conclusion

Forming an LLC in the United States can be a relatively simple process if done correctly from the start. The key is not just to incorporate the company, but to structure it properly according to your activity, tax residency, commercial objectives, and international strategy.

Having the correct documentation and understanding future obligations can avoid many problems down the road.
