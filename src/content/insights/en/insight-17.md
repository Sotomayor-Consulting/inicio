More and more Latin American entrepreneurs face an important decision when forming their LLC in the United States: use an automated platform like Stripe Atlas or hire personalized consulting?

Both options have advantages and disadvantages, and the right choice depends on your business model, knowledge, and goals.

In this article, we compare Stripe Atlas vs personalized consulting so you can make an informed decision.

## What is Stripe Atlas?

Stripe Atlas is a platform created by Stripe that allows you to form an LLC (or C-Corp) in Delaware in an automated way. It includes:

- Filing of Articles of Organization.
- Obtaining an EIN from the IRS.
- Stock issuance (if applicable).
- Stripe Treasury bank account.
- Access to a corporate management platform.

**Cost:** $500 USD (one-time payment).

### Advantages of Stripe Atlas

- **Fast and automated process:** Everything is managed from a digital platform.
- **Native Stripe integration:** Ideal if your business depends entirely on Stripe for payments.
- **Fixed and transparent cost:** You know exactly what you pay.
- **Basic documentation included:** Articles of Organization, EIN, standard Operating Agreement.
- **No need to talk to anyone:** The entire process is self-service.

### Disadvantages of Stripe Atlas

- **Only forms in Delaware:** Does not allow choosing other states like Wyoming or Florida, which could be more convenient for your case.
- **Generic templates:** The Operating Agreement is standard and does not adapt to your specific business needs.
- **No tax advice:** Does not include analysis of your tax residency, obligations, or planning.
- **No banking guidance:** Does not advise you on which bank to choose based on your profile.
- **Limited support:** If you have questions, support is generic and not personalized.
- **No structure analysis:** Does not evaluate if an LLC is the best option for your case or if another structure would be more suitable.

## When to choose Stripe Atlas?

Stripe Atlas can be a good option if:

- **Your business is simple** and does not need a complex structure.
- **You are sure Delaware is the right state** for your case.
- **Your business model depends 100% on Stripe** as a payment processor.
- **You have basic knowledge** of corporate and tax compliance.
- **Your budget is limited** and you prefer a low-cost solution.
- **You do not need tax advice** because you already have an accountant who understands your structure.

> **Example:** A software developer in Mexico selling SaaS to global clients using Stripe who already has an accountant handling taxes could be a good candidate for Stripe Atlas.

## When to choose personalized consulting?

Personalized consulting is the best option if:

- **You do not know which state to choose** and need guidance based on your case.
- **Your business has operations in Latin America** and needs international structuring.
- **You want to ensure compliance** with all tax obligations from the start.
- **You have multiple members** or plan to have a multi-member structure.
- **You need a bank account** at a specific bank (Mercury, Relay, etc.) and want guidance.
- **You value accompaniment** and having someone to ask when you have doubts.
- **You plan to grow** and need a structure that scales with your business.

> **Example:** A marketing agency in Colombia with three partners selling services to U.S. clients that needs tax, banking, and corporate structuring benefits more from personalized consulting.

## Common mistakes when choosing Stripe Atlas without consulting

### Delaware is not always the best option

Stripe Atlas only forms in Delaware, which has an annual Franchise Tax of $300. For many small businesses, Wyoming ($60 annually) or Florida ($138.75) are more economical options.

### Lack of tax planning

Many entrepreneurs use Stripe Atlas thinking the LLC will not pay taxes, without understanding that Delaware has its own rules and the IRS may require additional forms.

### Generic Operating Agreement

The standard Operating Agreement may not cover specific situations like multiple members, particular profit distribution, or exit clauses.

### No banking strategy

Stripe Atlas offers Stripe Treasury, but this may not be the best banking option for your business. You will not receive guidance on alternatives like Mercury or Relay.

## Can you combine both options?

Yes. Some entrepreneurs use Stripe Atlas for basic formation and then hire personalized consulting for tax and banking structuring. However, this can be more expensive than doing everything with one provider and may cause problems if the initial structure was not adequate.

Remember: the cost of a bad decision when forming your LLC can be much greater than the initial savings of using an automated platform.
